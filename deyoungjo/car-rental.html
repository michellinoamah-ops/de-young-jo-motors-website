What you'll need to rent

  • • A valid government-issued ID (national ID, international passport or driver's licence)
  • • A valid driver's licence for self-drive rentals (minimum 1 year held)
  • • Proof of address (utility bill or equivalent)
  • • A refundable caution deposit, held until the car is returned undamaged
  • • Minimum renter age of 23 for self-drive; no age limit for chauffeur-driven bookings

Self-drive or with a driver, your call

Most renters choose self-drive for full flexibility, or a chauffeur for airport runs, events and out-of-town trips where you'd rather not deal with Lagos traffic yourself. Both options include full insurance cover and 24-hour roadside support for the length of your rental.
