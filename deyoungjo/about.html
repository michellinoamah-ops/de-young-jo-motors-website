Our story

De Young Jo Motors started with a simple frustration: buying or renting a car in Nigeria shouldn't mean guessing whether the vehicle matches the description. We built our process around inspection first, honest pricing second, and fast communication throughout.

We've since grown into a full-service motor vehicle business: we deal in new and used cars, source and import vehicles on request, stock spare parts and accessories to keep them running, and offer rental, maintenance, servicing and repair support, all from the same two offices in Lagos.
